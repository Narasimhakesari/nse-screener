NSE Stock Agent

Today's best-scoring NSE stocks

The agent reads every actively traded NSE stock after each market close and scores it on six things: momentum, trend, company results, seasonality, news and swing setups. Pick how you invest and it re-ranks the list. Tap any stock to see why.

The market is cautious, so the tested model is holding cash. In the backtest, waiting at times like this cut the worst fall from about 30% to about 4%, for about 5% a year less return. Use this list to watch. If you buy anyway, use half-size positions and stop-losses.

    How to use it

    1. 1

      Choose your style

      Momentum is the default because it did best in testing. The other styles add trend, seasonality, chart setups or company results; each card shows how it did.

    2. 2

      Open a stock and read why

      Each stock shows what lifted its score, what to watch out for, its recent news and how it has done in the coming month in past years.

    3. 3

      Plan the trade, then review monthly

      Use the stop-loss and position-size calculator so one bad trade costs about 1% of your money. Re-check the list after each month-end; sell what drops out.

    Robustness checks and rules that failed

    Stocks that later delisted are included up to their last trading day. Five years includes a strong 2023 bull run, and momentum can fall hard when market leadership changes. Costs are 0.2% per round trip unless stated. Past results do not predict future ones.

    Not investment advice. This is a rules-based ranking from public data for education, not a recommendation to buy or sell, and not research by a SEBI-registered analyst. Prices are end-of-day, not live. Check the current price and the company's latest filings before you trade.
