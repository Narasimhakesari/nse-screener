How the technical score works

  • Trend, 55 points: price above 200-day average (20), 50-day above 200-day (12), 200-day rising (8), price above 50-day (10), price above 20-day EMA (5).
  • Momentum, 35 points: RSI(14) between 50 and 70 (10), MACD above its signal line and improving (10), 3- and 6-month return ranked against every stock in the screener (15).
  • Other, 10 points: within 10% of the 52-week high (7), 20-day volume above the 60-day average (3).

How the fundamental score works

Each stock with fundamentals is ranked against the other stocks that have them, on six measures, then weighted: return on equity 30%, 5-year profit growth 20%, 5-year sales growth 15%, P/E 15%, EV/EBITDA 12%, P/B 8%. Lower valuation ranks higher. EV/EBITDA is skipped for financial companies. A missing measure is left out and the rest re-weighted. Stocks without fundamentals are ranked on the technical score alone.

The Momentum 30 model

  • Universe: the 300 most-traded NSE stocks (60-day median turnover), priced ₹10 or more, with a year of history.
  • Rank: return from 12 months ago to 1 month ago, divided by the stock's volatility. This is the same idea as the published momentum indices.
  • Buy: the top 30, in equal amounts, while market breadth is rising.
  • Review once a month, after the last trading day: sell anything that has left the top 30 and buy what replaced it.
  • Sell everything if you follow the cautious version and market breadth turns down.
  • The 2 × ATR stop shown on each stock is optional protection. It was not part of the tested model.
  • Wait means above the 200-day average but not in the top 30. Avoid means below it.

How to read this. Stocks that later delisted are included up to the day they stopped trading, so survivorship bias is reduced, not removed. Past results do not predict future ones.

Data and limits

  • Universe: NSE main-board shares with at least 220 trading days of history, a price of ₹10 or more and median daily turnover of ₹2 crore or more. ETFs, SME listings and thinly traded shares are left out.
  • Prices: NSE end-of-day files, trading days. Backtests use files from Dec 2020. One-day drops of more than 25% are treated as splits, bonuses or demergers and earlier prices are scaled; each case is noted on the stock. Dividends are not adjusted.
  • Fundamentals: MoneyWorks4Me Nifty 50 list for those 50 stocks; others need the CSV import. Debt, cash flow, promoter holding and quarterly results are not included.
  • Company fundamentals for the Momentum 30 as of 14 Nov 2026 were read from Screener.in on that date: profit, debt, promoter pledging and FII and DII holdings. To refresh them, or to cover more stocks, upload a Screener.in export named fundamentals.csv to the repository; the next refresh merges it in.
  • Cautions are warning flags for you to read: quarterly profit below a year ago, losses, P/E above 80, debt above 1.5 times equity, pledged promoter shares above 10%, FIIs cutting their holding by more than one point, ROE and ROCE both under 10%, very high volatility, RSI above 75, delivery below 25%, estimated price adjustments, net selling in bulk or block deals on the last trading day, mixed news, and turnover under ₹10 crore. They do not change the Buy list. In the backtest, filtering the Momentum 30 by delivery percentage or by technical confirmation lowered returns, so no filter is applied.
  • News: checked by web search on 14 Nov 2026 for nine stocks only, chosen under earlier rules; most of the Momentum 30 are unchecked. Every other stock has a link to search its news. News does not change the score; read it before acting.
  • Smaller stocks move fast and can gap past a stop-loss. Size positions so one loss costs about 1% of capital.
