How to follow it

  1. Check this page after 8:30 pm on trading days, or get the list on Telegram (see the repository's README). Put about one fifth of your trading money into each new buy. If all 5 slots are full, there is nothing to buy.
  2. Buy at the open. In Kite you can place the order the night before as an AMO (after-market order) at market price; it fills at the open.
  3. Straight after buying, create one GTT. In Kite: Holdings, tap the stock, Create GTT, choose OCO (One Cancels Other). Stop-loss trigger: your buy price −8%, as a market order (with market protection) so it fills. Target trigger: your buy price +20%, as a limit order at that price. Type your real buy price into the ticket above and it works out both numbers.
  4. If neither hits within 30 trading days, sell at the open on the sell-by date (an AMO the evening before works) and delete the GTT. This page and the alert tell you when.
  5. Take every signal, including the ones that lose. About half the trades lose money. The rule earns from a few big winners; skipping trades or moving the stop-loss breaks it.
